Hotel Amrutha Sagar
📍 Haliyal Road (Near Tapovan Nagar)
250 m • 3 min walk

🥞 Your Daily Breakfast Lifesaver — Closest full-service veg restaurant right down the road from Krishna Hostel. Famous for piping hot Masala Dosa, Thatte Idli, Filter Coffee, and budget South/North Indian lunch thalis (₹70–120). Perfect mess alternative!

Ice Zone (Student Legend)
📍 Saptapur / Malmaddi
1.8 km • 5 min auto

🌯 The Legendary Paneer Roll Spot — Ask any student in Dharwad! Famous for massive, sauce-loaded Paneer Rolls, Veg Cutlets, Cold Shakes, and budget fast food. Costs ₹60–100 per heavy roll. Always buzzing in the evenings!

#StudentFavorite#PaneerRoll#LateEveningSnacks#BudgetBites
The Doorway Café
📍 Narayanpur, Dharwad
2.2 km • 6 min

☕ Top Hangout & Board Game Cafe — Aesthetic ambience with indoor & open-air seating, board games (Catan, Jenga, Uno), peri-peri fries, loaded burgers, thick shakes, and pasta. The go-to weekend hangout for hostel friend groups.

#CafeVibe#BoardGames#BurgersAndFries#WeekendChill
Basaveshwara Khanavali
📍 Near CBT / Court Circle
3.2 km • Direct Bus

🌾 Authentic North Karnataka Jolada Roti Oota — Craving wholesome home-style food? Unlimited Jowar Roti, spicy stuffed Ennegayi (brinjal), Shenga (peanut) chutney pudi, spicy saaru, fresh curd, and buttermilk for ₹100–120. A pure Dharwad cultural experience!

#JoladaRoti#NorthKarnatakaFood#UnlimitedThali#HealthyOota
Babu Singh Thakur Pedha & Girmit Adda
📍 Line Bazaar / Court Circle
3.0 km

🍬 The Original 175-Year Heritage Pedha — Started in 1846 at Line Bazaar. Rich caramelised mawa rolled in fine sugar. Take a box home to family! Right nearby in the evening, grab spicy Girmit (puffed rice with masala) & crispy Mirchi Bhajji for ₹20.

#HeritageSweets#ThakurPedha#GirmitBhajji#MustTakeHome
